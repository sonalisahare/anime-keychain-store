❓ Help Center

How can we help you today?

🛍️ How do I place an order?

Browse your favourite anime keychains, click Add to Cart, go to your cart, and click Buy Now to place your order.

🚚 How can I track my order?

Go to the Track Order page from the top menu and enter your Order ID to check your delivery status.

💳 Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (where available).

🔄 What is your return policy?

Products can be returned within 7 days if they are damaged or you receive the wrong item.

📞 Need more help?

Email us at support@animeverse.com or visit the Contact page. Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM.
